Firmware Upload

✓ Upload Received

The firmware binary has been written to the SD card as FIRMWARE.BIN.
Power-cycle or reboot the device to begin flashing.

What happens next

  1. On next boot, the SD bootloader detects FIRMWARE.BIN.
  2. Program flash is erased top-down (existing firmware survives until the last page).
  3. The new image is written row-by-row and verified.
  4. On success, FIRMWARE.BIN is deleted and the device resets into the new firmware.
  5. On failure, FIRMWARE.BIN is retained and the device boots the old firmware — retry is safe.

Do not power off during the flash window (~30–60 s after reboot). If the device becomes unreachable after reboot, allow 90 s before assuming a failure.
